7. Safety Tips

To ensure safe transactions, Tijarah recommends the following:

  • Verify Identity: Check seller/buyer profiles, ratings, and reviews before transacting
  • Meet Publicly: Conduct in-person exchanges in well-lit, public places during daylight hours
  • Inspect Products: Thoroughly inspect items before making payment
  • Secure Payments: Use secure payment methods and avoid wire transfers or cryptocurrency for unknown sellers
  • Protect Privacy: Never share sensitive personal information (bank details, passwords, ID numbers)
  • Trust Your Instincts: If something feels wrong, walk away from the transaction
